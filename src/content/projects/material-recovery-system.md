A custom polishing screen for a materials recovery facility. Its augers are shaped to separate appropriately sized 3D materials while preventing the plastic bag wrapping that plagued earlier versions.

The project went through multiple design iterations, both in the full assembly and in its individual subassemblies. My team and I learned what worked, what didn’t, and how to solve technical problems together. By the end of six months, we had become a close-knit team that trusted one another.
