In November 2016, I built a Captain America shield along with an electromagnetic gauntlet to hold it. The inspiration was a Hacksmith Industries video, made by the company I’d end up working for almost eight years later.

It’s still one of my favorite projects. Maybe because it was my first real thrill of bringing something from the movies into real life, or because of all the trips to the hobby store asking questions, or the look on my mom’s face when she saw it. Who knows.
