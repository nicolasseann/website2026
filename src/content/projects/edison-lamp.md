I designed and built this lamp for ME 103 (Product Realization, Design and Making) at Stanford. A gear ring sits on its wooden top, and curved metal panels wrap around the Edison bulb.

I wanted to make something I’d actually want in my own space. After failing to find a lamp I liked, I built one myself, starting with a single Edison bulb and designing everything else around it. From the beginning, I wanted the piece to emphasize walnut, steel and movement.

Building it took welding, woodworking, wood and metal turning, laser cutting and sanding, plus some electrical setup to light it. The finished lamp is walnut, mild steel and aluminum.
